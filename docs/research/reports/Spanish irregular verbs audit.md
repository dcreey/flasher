# Auditing Spanish irregular verbs: patterns, lists, and open conflicts

Spanish irregularity reduces to about twelve rule-driven families plus a closed list of roughly twenty individually irregular verbs, and nearly every error a conjugator can make falls into one of three traps: failing to propagate a base verb's pattern to its prefixed derivatives, applying a spelling rule inconsistently, or hard-coding a form where the Real Academia Española (RAE) allows two. The notes support complete, high-confidence coverage of stem-changing verbs (**about 490 verbs across nine patterns**, from single-page Wiktionary category lists) and of the fully irregular verbs (ser, ir, haber, estar, dar, ver, saber, caber, oír, reír, decir, hacer, andar; SpanishDict tables). Coverage is thinner for derivative families, accent-shifting -iar/-uar verbs, and defective verbs, where much rests on standard textbook knowledge the researchers could not check, because the RAE's dictionaries (rae.es, dle.rae.es) returned HTTP 403 for direct fetch. **Six conflicts need a product decision**: the future of predecir/contradecir/desdecir, the spelling of rio/rió, the primary yo form of roer/yacer/erguir, the status of balbucir and garantir, the scope of soler, and which -iar/-uar verbs take two accentuations. The strongest finding for audit design is that tests should be built from rules plus explicit exception lists, so that one error is caught across a whole family (all -tener, -poner, -venir, -traer, -ducir, -uir verbs) rather than verb by verb.

A note on sourcing. Cited items below carry a URL. Items marked **(unsourced)** are standard Spanish grammar that the research notes wrote from background knowledge and could not verify; they are included because an audit needs them, but they should be checked against the DLE or Nueva gramática (NGLE) before being used as ground truth. Confidence labels are High, Medium or Low.

## Stem changes: nine patterns and one decision rule

Stem-changing verbs split cleanly by infinitive ending. **-ar and -er verbs only diphthongize** (e-ie, o-ue), while **-ir verbs either raise (e-i) or diphthongize and also raise (e-ie-i, o-ue-u)**. Wiktionary's category pages give these counts: e-ie 188, o-ue 147, e-i 69, e-ie-i 68, o-ue-u 9, u-ue 5, i-ie 4, o-hue 2, e-ye 2 ([e-ie](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_e-ie_alternation), [o-ue](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_o-ue_alternation), [e-i](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_e-i_alternation), [e-ie-i](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_e-ie-i_alternation), [o-ue-u](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_o-ue-u_alternation)). The category lists include archaic entries (abeldar, estender, jeder, bolver, recebir), which a modern-Spanish app should skip. Confidence on membership is High for what is listed; the lists are high-coverage but not guaranteed complete (the notes flag that a few real verbs such as enhestar may be missing).

The diphthong appears only in stressed syllables (present indicative and subjunctive except nosotros/vosotros, and the tú/usted/ustedes imperatives). The -ir "raising" verbs also change in the preterite 3sg/3pl (sintió, durmió, pidió), the gerund (sintiendo, durmiendo, pidiendo) and the present subjunctive nosotros/vosotros (sintamos, durmamos, pidamos); -ar and -er stem-changers do not change in any of those cells (querer/quiso aside, which is a separate preterite irregularity). Verified example rows: sentir has siento, sintió, sintiendo, sintamos; dormir has duermo, durmió, durmiendo, durmamos ([sentir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/sentir), [dormir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/dormir)); pedir has pido, pidió, pidiendo, pidamos ([pedir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/pedir)); adquirir has adquiero but adquirimos, adquirió, adquiriendo ([adquirir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/adquirir)).

The e-ie lists (all from the Wiktionary category) are large. -ar verbs include acertar, apretar, atravesar, calentar, cerrar, comenzar, confesar, despertar, empezar, encerrar, enterrar, fregar, gobernar, helar, manifestar, negar, nevar, pensar, plegar, quebrar, recomendar, regar, sembrar, sentar, temblar, tropezar and their prefixed relatives (desterrar, desenterrar, recalentar, replegar, restregar, etc.). -er verbs are ascender, atender, cerner, defender, descender, encender, entender, extender, perder, querer, tender, verter, plus the whole tener family (abstener, atener, contener, detener, entretener, mantener, obtener, retener, sostener, tener). A handful of -ir verbs diphthongize without raising: cernir, discernir, concernir, hendir ([e-ie](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_e-ie_alternation)). The o-ue lists likewise: -ar verbs include acordar, acostar, almorzar (via forzar), aprobar, colar, colgar, comprobar, contar, costar, demostrar, encontrar, forzar, mostrar, probar, recordar, rodar, rogar, sonar, soñar, soltar, tostar, volar, volcar; -er verbs are absolver, cocer, conmover, demoler, devolver, disolver, doler, envolver, llover, moler, morder, mover, poder, resolver, revolver, soler, torcer, volver ([o-ue](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_o-ue_alternation)). Both lists carry derivatives that a conjugator should inherit rather than enumerate.

The e-ie-i group (-ir) comprises adherir, advertir, arrepentirse, asentir, conferir, consentir, convertir, desmentir, diferir, digerir, divertir, herir, hervir, inferir, ingerir, invertir, mentir, preferir, referir, requerir, sentir, sugerir, transferir and others, plus the **venir family** (advenir, avenir, convenir, contravenir, devenir, intervenir, prevenir, provenir, reconvenir, revenir, sobrevenir, subvenir, supervenir and rarer ones) ([e-ie-i](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_e-ie-i_alternation)). The e-i group has about 69 verbs: pedir, servir, seguir (and perseguir, conseguir, proseguir), repetir, vestir (and desvestir, investir, revestir, travestir), medir, elegir, corregir, regir, competir, concebir, derretir, despedir, expedir, gemir, impedir, rendir, decir and its -decir derivatives, and the ñ-verbs ceñir, desceñir, constreñir, estreñir, heñir, reñir, teñir, desteñir, reteñir ([e-i](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_verbs_with_e-i_alternation)). The notes flag that reír, sonreír, freír and desleír belong in this class (río, rio, riendo) but were absent from the category and are unsourced; treat them as Medium confidence and see the accent section below.

The small and closed groups are exact lists, which makes them easy to audit. **o-ue-u** is only dormir, morir, adormir, desmorir, entremorir, maldormir, premorir (and the pronominal dormirse, adormirse). **u-ue** is only jugar, rejugar, sobrejugar. **i-ie** is only adquirir, inquirir, perquirir, readquirir. **o-hue** is oler and desosar (huelo, deshueso). **e-ye** is errar. Any other verb receiving these patterns is an error.

Spelling changes stack on top of stem changes and are a frequent bug source. The Wiktionary tables confirm: forzar fuerzo, fuerce, forcé; comenzar comienzo, comience, comencé; colgar cuelgo, cuelgue, colgué; jugar juego, juegue, jugué ([forzar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/forzar), [comenzar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/comenzar), [colgar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/colgar), [jugar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/jugar)). Avergonzar combines three changes: avergüenzo (o-ue plus diaeresis to keep the g soft-u sound), avergonc- before e (avergoncé, avergonce, avergoncemos) and avergonzó ([avergonzar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/avergonzar)); agorar behaves the same way with ü (agüero, agüere, agoré) ([agorar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/agorar)). **Errar** is a documented conflict point: the DLE gives both "yerro o erro", "yerran o erran" and "yerre o erre", whereas Wiktionary attributes yerro to Spain and erro to Latin America, a regional split the DLE does not confirm ([DLE errar](https://dle.rae.es/errar), [Wiktionary errar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/errar)). Accept both; confidence in the regional claim is Low. **Erguir** has dual present forms, yergo/irgo, yergues/irgues, yergue/irgue, yerguen/irguen, subjunctive yerga/irga, but erguimos, erguís, and a preterite irguió/irguieron and gerund irguiendo that are always irgu- ([Wiktionary erguir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/erguir)).

For false friends, the notes establish by absence from every alternation category that the following are regular in the stem vowel: pretender, ofender, someter, comer, beber, deber, prender, aprender, vender, temer, meter, romper, pesar, cortar, robar, doblar, soplar, cobrar, colocar, innovar, pegar, recibir, escribir, partir, abrir, subir, vivir, discutir, sufrir, dirigir, escoger, ostentar, amueblar, absorber, mecer. This is Medium confidence (absence, not individual tables), but these are the verbs an over-eager stem-change rule would wrongly diphthongize. A related confirmed trap: coser (to sew) is regular while cocer (to cook) is o-ue with c-z (cuezo, cueza), and acordarse/recordar are genuine o-ue verbs.

## Irregular yo forms, -zco verbs and orthographic rules

**The -go verbs** are asir (asgo), caer (caigo), decir (digo), hacer (hago), oír (oigo), poner (pongo), salir (salgo), tener (tengo), traer (traigo), valer (valgo), venir (vengo), plus satisfacer (satisfago) and yacer (yago, yazgo, yazco) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)). Caber (quepo) and saber (sé) have their own yo forms, dar (doy), estar (estoy), ir (voy), ser (soy), haber (he) and ver (veo) are the remaining yo irregulars (standard, Medium-High). The present subjunctive is built from the yo form (salgo gives salga, never sala), and the irregular tú imperatives are di, haz, pon, sal, sé, ten, ve, ven ([Wiktionary appendix](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Spanish_verbs)). Prefixed compounds copy the base: mantener gives mantengo, mantuve, mantendré, mantén, mantenga ([mantener](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mantener)). The accent on mantén, detén, propón, convén comes from the base monosyllable, and (unsourced) shifts or disappears with attached pronouns.

**Where compounds diverge from the base** is the single most valuable audit list. Bendecir and maldecir keep the irregular yo (bendigo), preterite (bendije, bendijeron) and subjunctive (bendiga) but are **regular in the future and conditional (bendeciré, bendeciría) and in the tú imperative (bendice)**, and have a regular participle bendecido for compound tenses ([Wiktionary bendecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bendecir), [Wiktionary maldecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/maldecir)). Predecir, contradecir and desdecir keep digo/dije but take regular imperative -dice (predice, contradice, desdice, vos predecí) and have the future/conditional conflict discussed below ([predecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/predecir), [contradecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/contradecir), [desdecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/desdecir)). The hacer family takes deshaz and rehaz as imperatives, while satisfacer allows satisface or satisfaz ([satisfacer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/satisfacer)).

**The -zco rule** says verbs in -ecer, -acer, -ocer, -ucir add z before c when followed by o/a, so yo and the entire present subjunctive change: nacer nazco/nazca, crecer crezco, conocer conozco, ducir duzco ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)). Exceptions are **hacer and derivatives, mecer/remecer (mezo, meza), and cocer and derivatives (cuezo, cueza)**. The notes list ofrecer, agradecer, merecer, obedecer, establecer, aparecer, pertenecer, reconocer, complacer, amanecer, enriquecer, fortalecer and lucir/relucir as members (standard, Medium-High). The -ducir verbs (conducir, producir, traducir, reducir, introducir, seducir, deducir, inducir, aducir) add a j-preterite: conduje, condujiste, condujo, condujimos, condujisteis, condujeron, with the i dropped in -eron and in the imperfect subjunctive condujera, and a regular future (conduciré) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)).

**Orthographic changes** preserve the pronounced consonant. The Wikipedia page documents: tocar toquemos (c-qu), delinquir delincamos (qu-c), gozar gocemos (z-c), vencer venzamos (c-z), proteger protejamos (g-j), negar neguemos (g-gu), distinguir distingamos (gu-g), averiguar averigüemos (gu-gü), caer cayó/cayeron, construir construyó/construyeron, leer leímos, and the i-drop after ñ/ll: bullir bulló, tañer tañó ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)). Leer has leyendo, leyó, leyeron, leyera, leído ([Wiktionary appendix](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Spanish_verbs)). The y-glide applies to -aer, -eer, -oír, -oer and -uir verbs (traer trayendo, creer creyendo, oír oyendo, huir huyendo); -uir verbs additionally take y in present yo/tú/él/ellos (construyo) and no tilde on construiste/construimos (unsourced, standard). Verbs in -quir, -guir are not y-gliders (delinquió, siguió). The ñ/ll absorption list: tañer, atañer, bullir, mullir, zambullir, engullir, gruñir, bruñir, plus the e-i ñ-verbs ceñir (ciñó, ciñeron), reñir (riñó), teñir (tiñó), estreñir (unsourced for most, Medium).

**Accent-shift verbs** put the stress on the weak vowel in yo/tú/él/ellos of the present indicative and subjunctive and in the tú/usted/ustedes imperatives, never in nosotros/vosotros or the preterite. Wiktionary-verified: reunir reúno/reúne/reúnen, reunimos ([reunir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/reunir)); rehusar rehúso, rehusamos ([rehusar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/rehusar)); enviar envío ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)). The unsourced but standard lists are: -iar accented (enviar, guiar, variar, enfriar, fiar, criar, espiar, vaciar, liar, ampliar, desviar, confiar, resfriar, ansiar, esquiar, contrariar, fotografiar, piar, chirriar, rociar, aliar, averiar, ataviar, telegrafiar, porfiar); -uar accented (continuar, actuar, graduar, evaluar, situar, efectuar, acentuar, insinuar, habituar, exceptuar, puntuar, perpetuar, atenuar, fluctuar, devaluar, desvirtuar); and others (aunar, aislar, aullar, maullar, prohibir, cohibir, ahumar, ahusar, aupar, rehilar, descafeinar, ahijar, enraizar, airar). Regular contrasts that must not accent: cambiar, estudiar, copiar, limpiar, odiar, anunciar, apreciar, negociar, remediar, lidiar, presenciar, plagiar, silenciar, and every -guar verb (averiguo), plus peinar, reinar, bailar, cuidar, causar, pausar, ahuyentar. The monosyllabic preterites lose their tilde under the 2010 orthography: fie, fio, guie, guio, lie, lio, rio, hui, vio, dio, fue, fui ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs), Medium).

**Reír** is a compact audit case: río, ríes, ríe, reímos, reís, ríen; subjunctive ría, rías, ría, riamos, riáis (SpanishDict prints riais without accent), rían; preterite reí, reíste, rio, reímos, reísteis, rieron; gerund riendo; participle reído ([SpanishDict reír](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/reír)). Sonreír, freír, engreír, desleír and sofreír follow it, and freír has two participles, freído and frito ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)).

## Preterite, future, participle, gerund and imperative irregularities

**Strong preterites** use an irregular stem with unaccented endings -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron, and -eron after j. The stems are andar anduv-, estar estuv-, tener tuv-, poder pud-, poner pus-, saber sup-, caber cup-, haber hub-, querer quis-, venir vin-, hacer hic-/hiz-, decir dij-, traer traj-, -ducir -duj- (Medium-High, textbook; deshacer, bendecir, maldecir, predecir, contradecir, desdecir rows are Wiktionary-verified: [deshacer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/deshacer), [bendecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bendecir), [maldecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/maldecir)). Ser and ir share one preterite (fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron), and dar (di, dio) and ver (vi, vio) take regular -er/-ir endings without tildes ([SpanishDict dar](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/dar), [ver](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/ver)). **Derivative families inherit the stem**: -tener (abstener, atener, contener, detener, entretener, mantener, obtener, retener, sostener), -poner (anteponer, componer, contraponer, deponer, disponer, exponer, imponer, interponer, oponer, posponer, predisponer, presuponer, proponer, reponer, sobreponer, superponer, suponer, trasponer/transponer, yuxtaponer), -venir (advenir, avenir, contravenir, convenir, devenir, intervenir, prevenir, provenir, reconvenir, sobrevenir), -hacer (deshacer, rehacer, contrahacer), -decir (contradecir, desdecir, predecir, entredecir, interdecir, bendecir, maldecir), -traer (abstraer, atraer, contraer, detraer, distraer, extraer, retraer, sustraer, maltraer), -ducir (aducir, conducir, deducir, inducir, introducir, producir, reducir, reproducir, seducir, traducir, and others), and desandar (these derivative lists are unsourced from memory; Medium). Prever and proveer are not strong: preví, previó, previeron ([prever](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/prever)); proveí, proveyó, proveyeron ([proveer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/proveer)). Satisfacer is satisfice, satisficiste, satisfizo, satisficimos, satisficisteis, satisficieron; Wiktionary notes many speakers use regular satisfací, but the RAE prescribes the irregular forms ([satisfacer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/satisfacer)).

**Weak -ir preterite changes** happen only in 3sg/3pl and only in -ir verbs: pedir pidió/pidieron, sentir sintió, dormir durmió, morir murió, and every e-ie-i verb (preferir prefirió, mentir mintió, herir hirió). Querer and volver, being -er, do not change (quise is a strong stem, volvió regular). The y-glide verbs have tildes on 2sg/1pl/2pl (leíste, leímos, leísteis; caíste; oíste) and y in 3rd persons (leyó, cayeron, oyó, creyeron, proveyó) (unsourced for most, High confidence as textbook).

**Future and conditional stems** are irregular for twelve base verbs: decir dir-, hacer har-, poder podr-, poner pondr-, querer querr-, saber sabr-, salir saldr-, tener tendr-, valer valdr-, venir vendr-, caber cabr-, haber habr- (High; endings are regular -é/-ía; the Wiktionary-verified rows are for deshacer desharé, satisfacer satisfaré, and the SpanishDict tables for haber, caber, saber, decir, hacer). Derivatives inherit (contendré, compondré, convendré, desharé, sobresaldré, equivaldré, satisfaré). Satisfaceré and satisfacería are flagged as non-standard. Regular futures worth testing: bendeciré, maldeciré, preveré, proveeré, traeré, conduciré, andaré, estaré ([bendecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bendecir), [prever](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/prever), [proveer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/proveer)).

**Irregular past participles** are -to or -cho. The Spanish Wikipedia lists abrir abierto, absolver absuelto, cubrir cubierto, disolver disuelto, escribir escrito, morir muerto, poner puesto, resolver resuelto, romper roto, ver visto, volver vuelto, hacer hecho, decir dicho, with prefixed derivatives inheriting (descubierto, devuelto, envuelto, revuelto, compuesto, supuesto, deshecho, satisfecho, predicho, contradicho, desdicho, previsto, entrevisto) ([es.wikipedia Participio](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Participio)). Exceptions: **corromper is regular (corrompido)**, and bendecir/maldecir have regular verbal participles bendecido/maldecido. The doublets that are true verb doublets are exactly **freír (frito/freído), imprimir (impreso/imprimido) and proveer (provisto/proveído)**; adjective-only forms such as preso, confuso, atento, sujeto, electo and bendito/maldito are not compound-tense participles per the source, although SpanishDict also lists bendito for bendecir ([es.wikipedia Participio](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Participio), [SpanishDict bendecir](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/bendecir)). Participles of the fully irregular verbs are regular: sido, ido, habido, estado, dado, sabido, cabido, oído, reído, andado, valido ([SpanishDict](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/valer)). Unsourced extras (Medium): descrito, inscrito, prescrito, suscrito (also suscripto), proscrito, reabierto, entreabierto, desenvuelto, desprovisto, antevisto, revisto.

**Irregular gerunds** are of four kinds. First, -ir stem raising: sintiendo, durmiendo, muriendo, pidiendo, sirviendo, vistiendo, siguiendo, repitiendo, mintiendo, prefiriendo, and viniendo for venir, pudiendo for poder (SpanishDict-verified for the base irregulars; the rest unsourced, High). Second, i-to-y: yendo ([es.wikipedia Gerundio](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerundio)), leyendo ([Wiktionary appendix](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Spanish_verbs)), oyendo, trayendo, cayendo, creyendo, proveyendo, construyendo, huyendo, royendo, and derivatives. Third, i-absorption after ñ/ll: tañendo, bullendo, zambullendo, gruñendo, riñendo, ciñendo, tiñendo. Fourth, the unexpected non-changers: siendo, viendo, dando, andando, estando, sabiendo, cabiendo, habiendo, haciendo, diciendo, bendiciendo, prediciendo, riendo, yaciendo (spelled with c so no y). The notes treat irguiendo (erguir) as the only standard gerund; erguiendo is non-standard.

**Imperatives and fully irregular tables.** The eight irregular tú affirmatives are di, haz, ve, ten, pon, sal, sé, ven ([Wiktionary appendix](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Spanish_verbs)). Derivative accents: mantén, detén, contén, obtén, sostén; propón, compón, supón, expón, impón, dispón; prevén, convén, intervén; deshaz, rehaz; prevé, entrevé; but predice, contradice, bendice are regular ([SpanishDict contradecir](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/contradecir), [mantener](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/mantener), [prever](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/prever)). The vosotros affirmative is infinitive-minus-r plus d (comed, venid, decid, sed, id, ved, reíd, oíd), reflexives drop the d (levantaos) with the exception idos. Negative commands and usted/ustedes/nosotros commands use the present subjunctive, with the unique affirmative nosotros **vamos** (negative no vayamos) and reflexive vámonos (the vos and derivative-accent claims are unsourced, Medium; voseo edge cases for estar, ver and haber are Low). Haber has no usable imperative; SpanishDict lists he/habed, which the notes treat as archaic and unfit as test data ([SpanishDict haber](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/haber)). The negative imperative for dar is no des (tú) and no dé (usted); for ir no vayas; for ser no seas; for oír no oigas; for caber no quepas.

The full simple-tense tables for ser, ir, haber, estar, dar, ver, saber, caber, oír, reír, decir, hacer, andar (all persons, including -ra and -se imperfect subjunctives) come from SpanishDict and match standard RAE paradigms ([ser](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/ser), [ir](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/ir), [haber](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/haber), [estar](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/estar), [saber](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/saber), [caber](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/caber), [oír](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/oír), [decir](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/decir), [hacer](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/hacer), [andar](https://www.spanishdict.com/conjugate/andar)). An auditor should import these 13 tables wholesale as golden data. Three imperfect-indicative irregulars exist: era (ser), iba (ir), veía (ver, plus prever preveía, entrever, rever, antever); oía, reía, decía, cabía, había are regular. Two structural traps: ser and ir share the entire preterite and imperfect subjunctive, so tables must key by verb, not form; and oímos/reímos are identical in present and preterite.

## Defective, impersonal and variant verbs: where the RAE and the web disagree

The old rule held that defective verbs (abolir, agredir, transgredir) exist only in forms whose ending begins with i. The RAE has abandoned it for the best-known cases: **abolir, agredir and transgredir are now treated as complete, regular -ir verbs** (abolo, aboles, agredo), because forms outside the i-set are well documented ([DPD abolir](https://www.rae.es/dpd/abolir), [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763), [es.wikipedia Verbo defectivo](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbo_defectivo)). The still-defective set is small. Wiktionary's category lists exactly arrecir, aterir, balbucir, colorir, desvaír, embaír, empedernir and manir ([category](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_defective_verbs)); es.wikipedia adds acaecer, atañer, concernir, fallir, guarnir, usucapir, llover, nevar. For the i-only group the unsourced background rule (Medium) is that missing cells are yo/tú/él/ellos of the present, the whole present subjunctive and the tú/usted/ustedes imperatives; applies to aguerrir, arrecir, aterir, blandir, despavorir, empedernir, preterir, colorir, manir, usucapir. Status of these individual verbs in the current DLE is unverified.

**Balbucir** shows a source conflict. Castellano Actual (quoting the DPD) says it remains defective with no yo present or present subjunctive (use balbucear: balbuceo, balbucee), while es.wikipedia says the DPD now accepts -o forms as it did for abolir ([Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763), [es.wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbo_defectivo)). **Garantir** is similar: the DPD calls it rare and defective in most of the Spanish-speaking world but fully conjugated in Argentina and Uruguay ([DPD garantir](https://www.rae.es/dpd/garantir)); treat it as low priority and point to garantizar.

**Soler** is the clearest drill-design case. The DPD classifies it as irregular like mover (suelo, sueles; solía) and notes it is not used in future, conditional or imperative; the RAE student dictionary says it is used mostly in present indicative, present subjunctive and imperfect indicative ([DPD soler](https://www.rae.es/dpd/soler), [RAE student dictionary](https://www.rae.es/diccionario-estudiante/soler)). Wiktionary and conjugation sites such as 123teachme print a full table including soleré and solería, flagged "rare but acceptable" ([Wiktionary soler](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/soler), [123teachme](https://123teachme.com/spanish_verb_conjugation/soler)). The notes recommend following the RAE: drill present, imperfect and possibly preterite/perfect/present subjunctive, and grey out future, conditional and imperative.

**Impersonal and third-person-only verbs** split into weather verbs (llover o-ue, nevar e-ie, amanecer, anochecer, atardecer, granizar, lloviznar, helar, tronar), which use only 3sg literally but allow figurative personal use (Amanecí en Lima; llueven piedras) ([Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763)), and semantically restricted verbs (acaecer, acontecer, atañer, concernir, obstar, ocurrir, antojar), which take only 3sg/3pl ([Wiktionary category](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_third-person-only_verbs)). Haber's impersonal hay and gustar-type dative verbs (gustar, encantar, doler, parecer, faltar, interesar) are unsourced but standard; they are fully conjugable and drilled in 3sg/3pl.

**Variant forms to accept as sets**, with per-cell primaries:
- **roer**: yo roo / roigo / royo, subjunctive roa / roiga / roya ([es.wiktionary roer](https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/roer)); raer follows (unsourced, medium-high).
- **yacer**: yazco / yazgo / yago, imperative yace / yaz ([es.wiktionary yacer](https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/yacer)).
- **erguir**: see the stem section.
- **placer**: plazco, plazca; preterite 3sg plació / plugo, 3pl placieron / pluguieron; pluguiere is flagged archaic ([es.wiktionary placer](https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/placer)).
- **adecuar, licuar, evacuar**: adecuo / adecúo; the DPD accepts both, with the actuar pattern now the majority for evacuar ([DPD evacuar](https://www.rae.es/dpd/evacuar), [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/licuo-o-licuo-adecuo-o-adecuo-evacuo-o-evacuo/)).
- **satisfacer** imperative satisface / satisfaz.
- **valer** imperative vale (SpanishDict) or val (RAE accepts both per the notes, Medium).
- **-ra / -se** imperfect subjunctives (interchangeable).
- **predecir family** futures (below).

The dual -iar list (auxiliar, agriar, expatriar, repatriar, historiar, vidriar) is Low-Medium and unsourced beyond adecuar/licuar.

## Six open conflicts and how to resolve them

The table collects the disagreements that could produce false error flags in an audit. Where a source conflict exists, the recommended handling is to accept both until the DLE can be consulted directly.

| Issue | Source A | Source B | Recommended audit handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| predecir / contradecir / desdecir future and conditional | Wiktionary lists predeciré and prediré; SpanishDict lists contradiré first and contradeciré as alternate | Researcher recollection (unverified, RAE blocked): regular predeciré is the standard, prediré less common | Accept both; the notes disagree internally about which is primary, so mark neither as an error |
| 3sg preterite of reír, sonreír, freír, guiar, fiar | 2010 orthography: rio, fio, guio (no tilde), per SpanishDict and Wikipedia | Pre-2010 and common usage: rió, guió | Pick one primary (rio per the current norm) and decide whether tilded forms are errors or variants; product decision |
| errar, erguir, roer, yacer primary form | DLE lists both errar variants with no preference; es.wiktionary does not state a preference for roer | Wiktionary asserts yerro/erro regional split for errar | Accept all; do not apply the regional claim |
| balbucir | Still defective per Castellano Actual | DPD accepts -o forms per es.wikipedia | Treat as Low-confidence defective; accept both |
| soler scope | RAE: no future, conditional, imperative | Wiktionary, 123teachme: full table, rare forms acceptable | Follow RAE for drills; do not flag soleré as wrong in free input |
| -iar/-uar duals and "extra" variants (reunir, fiar, rehusar, ahincar) | adecuar, licuar confirmed dual | Wiktionary shows only reúno and rehúso; no source found for reunir or fiar duals | Treat reunir, rehusar, fiar as single-form unless a source appears |

Minor discrepancies inside the notes also warrant attention. The accent-shift file includes a stray "huis" in a list of -uir preterite forms where the correct monosyllabic form is huiste or the rule about hui. The note on satisfacer states that the RAE accepts only irregular preterite forms, while the same note says many speakers use regular forms, so an app should flag the regular forms as non-prescriptive rather than ungrammatical in free input. Valer's imperative is vale in SpanishDict and "val or vale" in the notes. The haber imperative he/habed is an artifact of the source and should be excluded. The notes also advise ignoring "hay" as a separate 3sg present cell for haber.

## Conclusion

The practical insight is that the notes cover roughly three levels of evidence, and an audit should treat them differently. Category-page lists of stem changers and the SpanishDict tables for the thirteen fully irregular verbs are strong enough to serve as golden test data now. Rule statements for orthography, -zco, y-glide and compound inheritance are textbook-standard and low risk, but the derivative lists (which prefixed verbs inherit which irregularity) were written from memory and should be validated programmatically, for example by checking every -tener, -poner, -venir, -traer and -ducir verb in the app against its base. The conflicts above are policy questions more than factual ones, since several forms are acceptable in the RAE's own listings.

The research left gaps that matter for completeness: no DLE or DPD page was reached, so RAE-primary claims (predecir futures, balbucir status, bendito as adjective, dual -iar verbs) remain unresolved; no individual conjugation tables were fetched for the regular false friends; vos and Latin-American variants were not extracted; pronominal-only verbs and meaning-change pairs were not researched; and haber impersonal habemos and the gustar-type verb lists are unsourced. Closing these with the DLE conjugation viewer and the NGLE would convert most Medium items to High and settle whether the six flagged conflicts are real disagreements or artifacts of secondary sources.
