## 1. -go yo verbs and derivative families (incl. prefixed-compound differences)

### Takeaway
Present-subjunctive stem = yo-form stem, so every -go verb has -ga subjunctive. Prefixed compounds copy the base verb (mantener like tener, componer like poner) with two systematic differences: the tú imperative gets an accent when the base was monosyllabic (ten -> mantén), and bendecir/maldecir are regular in future, conditional and tú imperative (but irregular in yo present, preterite, subjunctive). predecir/contradecir accept regular and contracted futures.

### Cited Findings
- G-verbs list: asir asgo, caer caigo, decir digo, hacer hago, oír oigo, poner pongo, salir salgo, tener tengo, traer traigo, valer valgo, venir vengo; satisfacer -> satisfago; yacer -> yago / yazgo / yazco — [Wikipedia: Spanish irregular verbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)
- Subjunctive built from yo present (salgo -> salga, not *sala); irregular tú imperatives: di, haz, pon, sal, sé, ten, ve, ven — [Wiktionary Appendix: Spanish verbs](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Spanish_verbs)
- hacer stem: hago, hice/hizo, haré, haría — same Wiktionary appendix.
- bendecir: bendigo, bendices, bendice, bendecimos, bendecís, bendicen; subj bendiga...; pret bendije, bendijiste, bendijo, bendijimos, bendijisteis, bendijeron; future bendeciré (regular), conditional bendeciría; imperative tú bendice, usted bendiga; gerund bendiciendo; participle bendecido or bendito — [Wiktionary bendecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bendecir)
- mantener: mantengo, mantienes; subj mantenga; pret mantuve, mantuvo, mantuvieron; future mantendré; imperative tú mantén (vos mantené), usted mantenga — [Wiktionary mantener](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mantener)
- satisfacer: satisfago; pret satisfice, satisficiste, satisfizo, satisficieron; future satisfaré; imperative tú satisface OR satisfaz; usted satisfaga; gerund satisfaciendo; participle satisfecho. Note: many speakers conjugate preterite/future/conditional/imperfect subjunctive as regular (satisfací, satisfaceré); RAE prescribes the irregular forms — [Wiktionary satisfacer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/satisfacer)
- predecir: future predeciré OR prediré; conditional predeciría OR prediría; imperative tú predice; pret predije; participle predicho — [Wiktionary predecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/predecir)
- contradecir: future contradeciré OR contradiré; conditional contradeciría OR contradiría; imperative contradice; pret contradije; participle contradicho — [Wiktionary contradecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/contradecir)
- Preterite i drops after j: traer -> trajeron, trajera (not *trajieron) — Wikipedia Spanish irregular verbs (above).

## 2. -zco verbs, -ducir, and exceptions

### Takeaway
Verbs in -ecer, -acer, -ocer, -ucir (vowel before -cer/-cir) add z before c when followed by o/a: yo -zco and entire present subjunctive -zca. Exceptions: hacer (hago) and derivatives, mecer (mezo), cocer (cuezo) and derivatives. -ducir verbs have -zco plus a j-preterite (conduje, condujeron) with no i.

### Cited Findings
- -zc- verbs: nacer nazco, crecer crezco, conocer conozco (and ducir duzco) — [Wikipedia: Spanish irregular verbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)
- Exceptions to -zc-: hacer and derivatives, mecer, remecer, cocer and derivatives — same page.
- yacer: yago, yazgo or yazco — same page.
- -ducir: preterite -duje, -dujiste, -dujo, -dujeron; imperfect subjunctive -dujera (i drops after j) — same page.

## 3. Orthographic changes (c-qu, g-gu, z-c, gu-gü, g-j, c-z, gu-g, qu-c, i-y, ñ/ll)

### Takeaway
Spelling changes preserve sound before e (subjunctive, preterite yo) or before o/a (g-j, c-z, gu-g). i->y between vowels (and in -uir) applies to 3rd-person preterite, gerund, imperfect/future subjunctive, plus yo/tú/él/ellos present for -uir; after ñ, ll (and j) the unstressed i of -ió/-ieron/-iendo/-iera disappears.

### Cited Findings
- c->qu: tocar -> toquemos; qu->c: delinquir -> delincamos — [Wikipedia: Spanish irregular verbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)
- z->c: gozar -> gocemos; c->z: vencer -> venzamos — same.
- g->j: proteger -> protejamos; mojar -> mojemos (no change, j kept) — same.
- g->gu: negar -> neguemos; gu->g: distinguir -> distingamos — same.
- gu->gü: averiguar -> averigüemos — same.
- caer: cayó, cayeron; construir: construyó, construyeron; -quir verbs are exceptions: delinquió, delinquieron; leer: leímos — same.
- ñ/ll: bullir -> bulló (not *bullió); tañer -> tañó — same.
- leer: stem y in gerund leyendo, pret leyó, leyeron, imperfect subj leyera; participle leído — [Wiktionary Appendix: Spanish verbs](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Spanish_verbs)

## 4. Accent patterns: -iar, -uar, and others; -eír verbs

### Takeaway
A subset of -iar/-uar verbs (and a few -aislar/-unir/-ibir/-usar verbs) stress the weak vowel in the "strong stem" forms: yo, tú, él, ellos in present indicative and subjunctive, and tú/usted/ustedes imperative; nosotros/vosotros forms and the preterite do not. The group is lexically listed. Most -iar/-uar verbs are regular (diphthong).

### Cited Findings
- reunir: reúno, reúnas... present indic reúno reúnes reúne reunimos reunís reúnen; subj reúna reúnas reúna reunamos reunáis reúnan; pret reuní reuniste reunió reunimos reunisteis reunieron; gerund reuniendo; imperative reúne / reúna — [Wiktionary reunir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/reunir)
- rehusar: rehúso rehúsas rehúsa rehusamos rehusáis rehúsan; subj rehúse...; imperative tú rehúsa, usted rehúse; vos rehusá — [Wiktionary rehusar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/rehusar)
- adecuar: dual present adecuo / adecúo, adecuas / adecúas, adecua / adecúa, adecuamos, adecuáis, adecuan / adecúan; subj adecue / adecúe...; imperative adecua / adecúa — [Wiktionary adecuar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/adecuar)
- enviar: envío (stress on i) — [Wikipedia: Spanish irregular verbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs)
- Per Wikipedia, criar, fiar, guiar, liar, piar "can be written with or without an accent: crie/crié, fie/fié" — this is the monosyllabic preterite orthography (see Inferences), not a stress difference; licuar and adecuar allow both adecuo and adecúo — same page.
- reír/freír: río, ríe, ría; pret rieron (single i); monosyllable perfect forms like rio written without accent; freír has two participles freído or frito — same page.
- Monosyllables: no tilde in fie, fio, guie, guio, lie, lio, rio, hui, huis, vio, dio, fue, fui (RAE 2010 orthography; MED, general knowledge)

## 5. Verbs with two accepted conjugations and regular verbs that look irregular

### Takeaway
Confirmed dual forms: adecuar (adecuo/adecúo), licuar (per Wikipedia), predecir/contradecir futures, satisfacer imperative (satisface/satisfaz), satisfacer regular-vs-irregular preterite in usage, vos/tú variants. -iar "dual" in the sense of fie/fié is orthographic, not stress. Many things that look irregular are only spelling adjustments or fully regular.

### Cited Findings
- adecuar and licuar allow adecuo and adecúo — [Wikipedia: Spanish irregular verbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_irregular_verbs); [Wiktionary adecuar](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/adecuar)
- freír participle freído or frito — Wikipedia (above). bendecir participle bendecido or bendito — [Wiktionary bendecir](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bendecir)
- satisfacer tú imperative satisface or satisfaz — [Wiktionary satisfacer](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/satisfacer)
- predecir / contradecir future and conditional dual — Wiktionary predecir, contradecir (above).
- yacer yago/yazgo/yazco — Wikipedia (above).
- -ra and -se imperfect subjunctive interchangeable — [Wiktionary Appendix: Spanish verbs](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Spanish_verbs)

### Inferences
- Other dual forms (MED, general knowledge): valer imperative val/vale; roer yo roo/roigo/royo; hacer-compounds imperative -haz only; leer none; cabe none; sobresaltar none; imperfect subjunctive -ra/-se for every verb; nosotros "vamos"/"vayamos"; prever/proveer participles; proveer proveído/provisto; imprimir impreso/imprimido; sustituir fine; erguir yergo/irgo (erguir: yergo/irgo; yergue/irgue; pret irguió, irguieron; LOW-MED).
- Regular-looking-irregular (should pass a "regular" test, only spelling-adjust): pagar, tocar, cruzar, coger, vencer, seguir, averiguar, leer, creer, construir (y-insertion is predictable), tañer/bullir (i-drop predictable), sonreír, cocer-vs-coser (coser is regular), cerrar (stem change, not -go).
- Irregular-looking-regular: andar (regular present, irregular preterite anduve), ver (veo; imperfect veía), prever (preveo, previó, prevé, prevea), ir/dar/ser pret fui/di have no tilde, oler (huelo with h-), haber vs hay, tener imperative ten vs mantén.
- Defective/odd: abolir (only forms with i), placer (plazco etc., plugo), soler.
- Practical test guidance: for items marked "dual", accept both; for "-iar dual" accept both stressed and unstressed only for the listed verbs (auxiliar, agriar, expatriar, repatriar, historiar).

### Gaps
- No authoritative RAE list of dual -iar/-uar verbs reached; dual list above is LOW-MED beyond adecuar/licuar.
- No source found for reunir/fiar/ahincar/rehusar duality asserted in the brief (see section 4 gaps).
