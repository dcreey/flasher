## 1. Defective verbs and which forms exist

### Takeaway
RAE's current position: the old "only forms whose ending starts with i" defectives (abolir, agredir, transgredir, and now garantir in most of the Spanish-speaking world) are mostly treated as complete and regular in the DPD/DLE; truly defective today are soler (tense-restricted), balbucir (no 1sg present / present subjunctive), and a handful of archaic verbs (arrecir, aterir, empedernir, colorir, manir, etc.). For an app: soler is fully conjugable in practice but should be drilled only in present, imperfect (and preterite/perfect/pres. subjunctive as secondary); don't drill future/conditional/imperative.

### Cited Findings
- soler: RAE DPD says irregular like mover; main forms are present and imperfect indicative (suelo, sueles; solía, solías); not used in future, conditional, imperative. — [DPD soler (via search summary)](https://www.rae.es/dpd/soler)
- soler: RAE's Diccionario del estudiante says it is used mostly in present indicative, present subjunctive, and imperfect indicative. — [RAE Diccionario del estudiante](https://www.rae.es/diccionario-estudiante/soler)
- soler: always followed by an infinitive (explicit or implicit) to express habitual action; stem has diphthong ue in stressed forms (suelo) vs. solía. — [RAE Glosario: verbo defectivo](https://www.rae.es/gtg/verbo-defectivo)
- soler (Wiktionary): present-tense use is main; "used to" in imperfect; infinitive, gerund (soliendo), participle (solido), preterite, future, subjunctive, conditional, imperative are very rare but acceptable; future and conditional footnoted "rare but acceptable"; future subjunctive chiefly legal language. — [Wiktionary soler](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/soler); contradicts RAE which excludes future/conditional/imperative, so follow RAE for drill — [DPD soler](https://www.rae.es/dpd/soler)
- abolir: traditionally defective (only forms with ending beginning in i: abolimos, abolís, abolía, abolió, abolir...), but DPD says today the remaining forms are documented and valid ("se abole la pena de muerte"); it is regular: abolo, aboles (not "abuelo"). — [DPD abolir](https://www.rae.es/dpd/abolir); [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763)
- agredir, transgredir: no longer defective in DLE 22nd ed. (2001), i.e. full regular conjugation (agredo, transgreden). — [es.wikipedia Verbo defectivo](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbo_defectivo); [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763)
- garantir: DPD says rare; defective in most of the Spanish-speaking world (only i-ending forms), but used in all forms in Argentina and Uruguay; garantizar has replaced it. A Castellano Actual piece (based on 2005 DPD) says it can be conjugated fully ("le garanto"). — [DPD garantir](https://www.rae.es/dpd/garantir); [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763) (sources differ on severity; treat garantir as low-priority)
- balbucir: still defective; no 1sg present indicative or present subjunctive forms ("balbuzco/balbuzo/balbuzca" avoided); balbucear fills the gap (yo balbuceo, que yo balbucee). The es.wikipedia article says the DPD now accepts -o forms (as with "abolo"), which conflicts with Castellano Actual saying still defective. — [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763); contradicted in part by [es.wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbo_defectivo)
- Wiktionary's "Spanish defective verbs" category lists exactly: arrecir, aterir, balbucir, colorir, desvaír, embaír, empedernir, manir. — [Wiktionary category](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_defective_verbs)
- es.wikipedia names as defective: acaecer, atañer, balbucir, colorir, concernir, empedernir, fallir, guarnir, llover, nevar, usucapir. — [es.wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbo_defectivo)
- Abolir, agredir, transgredir change was driven by growing use of forms whose ending does not begin with i. — [es.wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbo_defectivo)

### Inferences
- Suggested app tiers: (A) soler: conjugate present/imperfect/preterite/perfect/present subjunctive, grey out future, conditional, imperative (match RAE); (B) abolir, agredir, transgredir: treat as fully regular -ir verbs; (C) balbucir, arrecir/arrecirse, aterir/aterirse, empedernir, colorir, manir, usucapir, blandir, braguir, despavorir, preterir, aguerrir, guarnir, fallir: restrict to i-ending forms (see Gaps for the form set); (D) garantir: mark as rare, redirect to garantizar.
- Because Wiktionary's table lists every soler tense, if the app borrows from Wiktionary it must filter by RAE.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch DPD/DLE entries for blandir, braguir, colorir, despavorir, empedernir, aguerrir, arrecirse, aterirse, manir, preterir, usucapir, desolar, so their status is not source-verified. Unverified background knowledge (medium confidence): traditional rule "only forms whose ending begins with i" (infinitive, gerund -iendo, participle -ido, 1pl/2pl present -imos/-ís, imperfect -ía, preterite -í/-iste/-ió/-imos/-isteis/-ieron, future -iré, conditional -iría, imperfect subjunctive -iera/-iese, future subjunctive -iere, imperative vosotros -id; forms with stem+e/a/o missing: yo present, tú/él/ellos present, whole present subjunctive, tú/usted/ustedes imperatives). Applies to aguerrir, arrecir, aterir, blandir (also braguir, brandir variants), despavorir, empedernir, preterir, colorir, manir, usucapir, desolar (desolar is actually an o-ue verb; 'desolar' rarely defective, unsure).
- Whether DLE currently marks colorir/despavorir/empedernir/manir defective and whether aterirse/arrecirse are pronominal-only: unverified.

## 2. Impersonal and third-person-only verbs

### Takeaway
Weather verbs (llover, nevar, amanecer, anochecer) are used only in the 3rd person singular with no subject in literal use, but allow personal/metaphorical use ("Amanecí en Lima"). Acaecer, acontecer, atañer, concernir, ocurrir, obstar, antojar are third-person-only (3sg/3pl) because of semantics.

### Cited Findings
- Weather verbs llover, amanecer, anochecer: used only in third person, with no grammatical subject ("Llueve todas las madrugadas"); metaphorical use allowed ("Amanecí en Lima"). — [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763)
- atañer, concernir, acontecer, acaecer, holgar, ocurrir: defective for semantic/syntactic reasons; take only inanimate/abstract subjects and only 3rd person; *yo ataño, *tú acaeces not acceptable. — [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/?p=763)
- Wiktionary "third-person-only verbs" category: acaecer, acontecer, antojar, atañer, concernir, obstar, ocurrir. — [Wiktionary category](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_third-person-only_verbs)
- es.wikipedia lists acaecer, atañer, concernir, llover, nevar as defective; "nadie dice acaezo". — [es.wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbo_defectivo)

### Inferences
- App: model three classes — (1) strictly impersonal (weather: llover o>ue, nevar e>ie, granizar, lloviznar, amanecer, atardecer, anochecer, relampaguear, tronar o>ue, helar e>ie, diluviar, escampar, ventear; and haber impersonal "hay"); only 3sg forms; (2) third-person-only (acontecer, acaecer, atañer, concernir e>ie, ocurrir, suceder, bastar, obstar); 3sg/3pl; (3) gustar-type dative verbs (gustar, encantar, doler o>ue, molestar, parecer, faltar, sobrar, quedar, interesar, apetecer, antojarse, convenir, tocar, fascinar, aburrir, disgustar) which are fully conjugable but in practice drilled in 3sg/3pl with an indirect-object pronoun.
- Weather verbs with figurative personal use (amanecer, anochecer, atardecer, llover "llueven piedras", relampaguear) should keep a flag allowing full forms.

### Gaps
- No source fetched for gustar-type verb lists, haber impersonal ("hay"/"habemos" regional), or irregular patterns of weather verbs; the stem-change patterns above are unverified background knowledge (high confidence for llover/nevar/helar/tronar; medium for others). Convenir is conjugated like venir (convenga, convino) — background, high confidence. Dative-verb forms (me gustan, etc.) not sourced.
- Does RAE label diluviar, escampar, ventear impersonal? Not verified.

## 3. Verbs with two conjugation patterns / two valid forms

### Takeaway
roer, yacer, erguir, placer have documented alternates; adecuar/evacuar/licuar have two valid accentuations.

### Cited Findings
- roer: yo roo / roigo / royo; present subjunctive roa / roiga / roya (roig- also roigas, roigamos; roy- also royas, royamos). — [es.wiktionary roer](https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/roer)
- yacer: yo yazco / yazgo / yago; subjunctive yazca / yazga / yaga; imperative tú yace / yaz. — [es.wiktionary yacer](https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/yacer)
- erguir: present yergo/irgo, yergues/irgues, yergue/irgue, erguimos, erguís, yerguen/irguen; present subjunctive yerga/irga (nosotros irgamos, vosotros irgáis); preterite erguí, erguiste, irguió, erguimos, erguisteis, irguieron; imperative tú yergue/irgue. — [es.wiktionary erguir](https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/erguir)
- placer: present plazco, subjunctive plazca; preterite 3sg plació / plugo, 3pl placieron / pluguieron; imperfect subjunctive placiera/placiese/pluguiera/pluguiese; future subjunctive placiere/pluguiere (flagged archaic). plega/plegue not listed on the page. — [es.wiktionary placer](https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/placer)
- adecuar, evacuar, licuar: DPD says both adecúo and adecuo valid (like actuar vs. averiguar); evacuar: cult preference as averiguar but actuar-pattern now majority and valid; Castellano Actual suggests stress-less in Spain, accented in America (style recommendation). — [DPD evacuar](https://www.rae.es/dpd/evacuar); [Castellano Actual](https://www.udep.edu.pe/castellanoactual/licuo-o-licuo-adecuo-o-adecuo-evacuo-o-evacuo/)

### Inferences
- Model these as variant sets per cell: for roer/yacer/erguir, drill should accept all variants and mark one primary. Suggested primary: roo (roer), yazco (yacer), yergo (erguir) (background judgment).
- Wiktionary's roer page "does not say which is preferred".

### Gaps
- Unverified (background knowledge): raer: rao / raigo / rayo with matching subjunctives (same as roer, medium-high confidence); asir: asgo, asga (high); caer: caigo, caiga, cayó, cayera; cocer o>ue with zc/z spelling (cuezo, cuezas); ahincar: ahínco; ahijar: ahíjo, aíslo-type accents; agorar: agüero (ü); desosar: deshueso/ deso- (h- inserted; background); freír: participles freído (RAE prefers) / frito; imprimir: impreso / imprimido (impreso preferred); proveer: provisto/proveído; pronominal details. Not sourced here, so confirm via DLE before use.
- placer plega/plegue/pluguiere: only plugo/pluguiere sourced; plegue/plega not confirmed.

## 5. Auxiliary-like verbs (soler, poder, deber, querer, saber, haber de)

### Takeaway
Only soler's auxiliary role is sourced.

### Cited Findings
- soler is always followed by an infinitive, expressing habitual action; Wiktionary labels it an "auxiliary verb" expressing habit/tendency. — [RAE Glosario](https://www.rae.es/gtg/verbo-defectivo); [Wiktionary soler](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/soler)

### Inferences
- Poder is irregular (o>ue, pude, podré), deber is regular, querer is e>ie (quise, querré), saber irregular (sé, supe, sabré) — these are well-known irregularities (background, high confidence; no source retrieved). All combine with infinitives; haber de + infinitive is an obligation/necessity perífrasis.

### Gaps
- No source retrieved for modal/auxiliary patterns.

## 7. How other resources label defective verbs

### Takeaway
Wiktionary uses "Spanish defective verbs" and "third-person-only verbs" categories; RAE uses "verbo defectivo" in DLE and Glosario and has reduced the defective set over time; some sites (123teachme) show soler future/conditional contrary to RAE.

### Cited Findings
- Wiktionary: separate categories for defective (8 verbs) and third-person-only (7 verbs); soler shown with full table plus "rare" footnotes. — [Wiktionary defective](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_defective_verbs); [third-person-only](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Spanish_third-person-only_verbs); [soler](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/soler)
- A conjugation site (123teachme) shows soler future soleré and conditional solería, contrary to RAE. — [DPD soler summary / 123teachme](https://123teachme.com/spanish_verb_conjugation/soler)
- RAE Glosario defines verbo defectivo and uses soler as the example. — [RAE Glosario](https://www.rae.es/gtg/verbo-defectivo)
- Academic note: Spanish abolir and brandir share the same defectiveness pattern. — [Colmex NRFH article on abolir](https://revistas.colmex.mx/Record/oai:oai.nrfh.colmex.mx:article-3714/Description?lng=en)

### Inferences
- For consistency, mirror RAE (not Wiktionary tables) for soler and treat Wiktionary categories as a cross-check list of "truly defective" verbs.
