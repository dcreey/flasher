# Verification of unverified Mexican slang, sayings and Spain-side equivalents

Method: DEM (Diccionario del Español de México, El Colegio de México) entry pages fetched at https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/<word> on 2026-11-07, and the entry text read directly. DEM register labels: *(Popular)* = colloquial/informal, *(Groser)* = vulgar/coarse, *(Ofensivo)* = offensive, unmarked = neutral/general. Spain-side items were checked against English Wiktionary raw entries (region labels such as "Spain", "colloquial"). The Centro Virtual Cervantes Refranero could not be used: every cvc.cervantes.es URL redirected to a "mantenimiento" (maintenance) page on that date. The Academia Mexicana de la Lengua's dictionary was not reached. Sayings that have no DEM entry were checked against Mexican press and learner sources, which are weaker evidence and are flagged as such.

Verdicts: **Confirmed** = an authoritative dictionary has the item with the meaning given in the notes. **Confirmed + correction** = it exists, but the notes' meaning or register needs a change. **Weak** = only press, learner or popular sources. **Unverified** = nothing found.

## Which items are confirmed (with register), corrected, or still unverifiable?

### Takeaway
DEM confirms most of the Mexican slang and verb items, usually labelled *(Popular)*. Exceptions: *pendejo* and *encabronarse* are *(Groser)*; *a la chingada* is *(Groser y Ofensivo)*; *¡Bueno!* on the phone, *de pilón* and *dar lata* are unmarked (neutral). *Sale*, *va*, *no manches*, *voltear a ver*, *hablarle* (= phone) and *hacer la vaquita* have no DEM sense. The refranes about the nopal, the perico and the mole de olla appear only in press and popular sources.

### Inferences
- In DEM, most of this Mexican slang carries *(Popular)*, the same label as ordinary colloquial words. So "safe, informal" is a fair register in the notes for chale, cañón, varo, feria, sacar de onda, estar hasta el gorro, valer gorro, aventón and echar los perros.
- Wiktionary's region labels are lighter than a national dictionary's. A missing "Spain" label (flipar, estar al loro, quinto pino) does not mean the item is pan-Hispanic. It only means the label could not be confirmed.

### Gaps
- The CVC Refranero multilingüe was down for maintenance on 2026-11-07, so no Mexican variant listings could be checked for the refranes.
- The Academia Mexicana's *Diccionario de mexicanismos* (2010) was not reached online. It is the next source to try for sale, va, no manches, voltear a ver, hablarle (phone), hacer la vaquita and ¿jalas?.
- DLE (rae.es) could not be reached, as before. *Me importa un pimiento* still lacks a dictionary citation.

## Do any meanings or registers in the notes need correction?

### Takeaway
Yes, eight small changes: spell the greeting *¿qué transa?*; label *pendejo* vulgar rather than "offensive"; add a caution for *chale*; add the extra senses of *estar hasta el gorro* and *cañón*; narrow *valer gorro*; mark *va* = OK as shared with Spain; and note that *echar los perros* = flirt is DEM-confirmed even though Wiktionary disagrees.

### Cited Findings
- ¿Qué tranza? should be spelled "¿Qué transa?". The notes' item 111 already cites "transa", while item 4 uses "tranza". — [DEM transa](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/transa)
- The notes call *pendejo* "vulgar-offensive". DEM labels it *(Groser)* only, but it is still the strongest-register entry in this set after *chingada*. — [DEM pendejo](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/pendejo)
- *chale* as a noun is a slur for a Chinese person (Popular y Ofensivo). Worth a caution note. — [DEM chale](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/chale)
- *Estar hasta el gorro* can also mean "very drunk or high", not only "fed up". — [DEM gorro](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/gorro)
- *Valer gorro*: DEM gives only "matter very little". The notes' "be worthless / be ruined (ya valió)" is sourced only for *valer madre*. — [DEM gorro](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/gorro)
- *Cañón* also has a Caló sense, "drunk". — [DEM cañón](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/cañón)
- *Va* = okay is labelled for Mexico **and** Spain, so it is not a Mexico-only item. — [Wiktionary va](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/va)
- *Echar los perros* = flirt: DEM confirms it (Popular). This settles the open question in verbs.md row 73, where Wiktionary's only gloss was "to come down on". — [DEM perro](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/perro); [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/echar_los_perros)
- *¡Bueno!* (phone), *de pilón* and *dar lata* are unmarked in DEM. They are neutral, so a register of "neutral" is right. — [DEM bueno](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/bueno); [DEM pilón](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/pilón); [DEM lata](https://dem.colmex.mx/Ver/lata)

### Inferences
- *¿Jalas?* follows naturally from DEM *jalar* I.5 (Popular). The meaning in the notes is probably right, but the exact form is still uncited.

### Gaps
- For the refranes about the nopal, the perico and the mole de olla, no lexicographic source was found. The meanings in the notes match the press and popular sources.
- *Hablarle* = phone someone, *voltear a ver* and *no manches* have no authoritative dictionary support in the sources reached.
